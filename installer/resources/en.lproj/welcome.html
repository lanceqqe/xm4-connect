XM Connect

Control your Sony WH-1000XM5 from the Mac menu bar, with a live 3D headset that reacts to every setting.

  • Noise cancelling, ambient sound and Focus on Voice
  • Equalizer with presets and your own curve
  • Speak-to-Chat, DSEE Extreme, pause when taken off, auto power off
  • Battery level, power off

The app is installed in Applications and opens in the menu bar when installation finishes.
